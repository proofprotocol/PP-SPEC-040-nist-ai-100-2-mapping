# Contributing

PP-SPEC-040 is maintained as part of the Proof Protocol specification series.

Contributions may be submitted as issues or pull requests.

Contributions should preserve Proof Protocol's framework-agnostic architecture, distinguish NIST AI 100-2 concepts from Proof Protocol concepts, identify sources clearly, avoid unnecessary reproduction of upstream material, and preserve the normative meaning of the specification.

By contributing, you represent that you have the right to submit the contribution.
